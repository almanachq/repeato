Repeato — foreground service (SPECIAL_USE)

Demonstration for the Google Play foreground service permission declaration (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE).

The recording shows an "alarm"-style reminder the user scheduled. With the app in the background and the phone locked, the reminder fires at its exact time: a foreground service plays the alarm tone and vibration and keeps the full-screen alarm on screen until the user dismisses it. Repeato holds SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM; this is the Play policy scenario for apps that use a foreground service to continue alarms in the background.
