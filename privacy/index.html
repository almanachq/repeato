Repeato Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Repeato runs entirely on your device. It has no servers, no account, and no internet permission. It collects nothing, transmits nothing, and shares nothing.

What Repeato stores

Repeato saves your reminders, their history, and your app settings in its own private storage on the device. Android removes all of it when you uninstall the app. None of it leaves the device.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Repeato asks for it
NotificationsShow a reminder when it fires.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)Fire each reminder at the exact time you set.
Full-screen notificationsLet an alarm-style reminder take over the screen over the lock screen.
Run at startupRe-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
Wake lock, vibrate, foreground serviceRing an alarm reliably until you respond.
CalendarOnly used when you turn on “Add to calendar” for a reminder. Repeato writes that one event and updates or removes it with the reminder. It never reads your other calendar entries.

Backups

You can export your reminders to a JSON or CSV file from Settings. Repeato writes that file only where you choose to save it and never uploads it. Repeato opts out of Android system backup (allowBackup="false"), so your data is not copied to Google Drive or transferred between phones automatically.

Third parties

Repeato contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Children

Repeato collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
